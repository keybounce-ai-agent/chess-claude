How to play

Select a ply in the timeline, then move on the Now board. The newest ply is the present move. Your own earlier plies (dashed outline) can be edited for energy equal to your turns back. An edit sends a timewave forward, 3 plies per turn, recomputing each position. A recorded move that becomes illegal must be replaced by its owner. Red squares show where Now and Projected differ. Hot-seat: both players share this screen.
